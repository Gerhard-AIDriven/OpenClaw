Here is the general format for querying the Titles layer:

https://data.linz.govt.nz/services/query/v1/vector.json?key=b2e35aafd4e848e9b0265f1caf575255&layer=50804&x=[x]&y=[y]&max_results=3&radius=10000&geometry=true&with_field_names=true

Replace [x] and [y] with the proper coordinates.

https://data.linz.govt.nz/services/query/v1/vector.json?key=b2e35aafd4e848e9b0265f1caf575255&layer=50804&x=176.9038079&y=-39.5006425&max_results=3&radius=10000&geometry=true&with_field_names=true
