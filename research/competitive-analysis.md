# Competitive Analysis: Property Due Diligence Report

**Date:** 2026-08-04  
**Market:** New Zealand Property Intelligence Services  
**Prepared by:** AI Driven (Gerhard Stimie)

## Executive Summary

The NZ property due diligence market is **bifurcated**:
- **High-end, slow, expensive:** Formal LIMs ($300-$450, 5-10 days)
- **Low-end, basic, free:** Trade Me/QV property search (surface-level data only)

**White Space Opportunity:** Mid-market screening tool ($75-$200, instant-4 hours) that provides 70% of LIM depth at 25% of the cost and 95% faster delivery.

**AI Driven's Competitive Advantage:** Speed + Affordability + Accessibility for the **screening phase** (before formal LIM).

## Competitor Landscape

### Tier 1: Formal LIM Providers (Direct Legal Substitute)

#### 1. Local Councils (e.g., Napier City Council, Hastings District Council)
**Product:** Land Information Memorandum (LIM)  
**Price:** $300-$450 NZD  
**Turnaround:** 5-10 working days (standard), 2-3 days (urgent, +50% fee)  
**Market Position:** Monopoly provider under Building Act 2004

**What They Offer:**
- ✅ Legally protected report (Building Act 2004)
- ✅ Complete building consent history (approved/declined/CCC status)
- ✅ Outstanding council notices (enforcement, repairs, compliance)
- ✅ Detailed drainage plans (as-built records)
- ✅ All known hazards (including unmapped issues)
- ✅ Contaminant history from council files
- ✅ Utility connection details (water, sewer, stormwater)
- ✅ Road proposals affecting property
- ✅ Certificate of title (current as of report date)

**What They DON'T Offer:**
- ❌ Investment analysis or rental yield projections
- ❌ Market comparables or valuation estimates
- ❌ Development potential assessment (beyond zoning confirmation)
- ❌ Fast turnaround (cannot be automated - manual file review)
- ❌ Digital-first experience (PDF via email, no interactive portal)
- ❌ Screening discounts (same price whether you buy or not)

**Weaknesses:**
- Slow (5-10 days kills deal momentum)
- Expensive for screening multiple properties
- No investment insights (just raw data)
- Poor UX (paper-based processes in many councils)
- Cannot be used for "what-if" scenarios

**Their Moat:**
- **Legal monopoly** - Only they can issue legally protected LIMs
- **Exclusive access** to non-digitized council files
- **Building Act mandate** - Banks/lawyers require formal LIM for settlement

### Tier 2: Commercial Property Data Platforms

#### 2. QV (Quotable Value) - State-Owned Enterprise
**Product:** QV Property Reports, QV e-Reports  
**Price:** 
- Free basic property search (address, CV, land area)
- $15-$30 for detailed sales history
- $50-$100 for full property report (rental estimates, sales history)
- $300+ for registered valuation

**Website:** qv.co.nz  
**Market Position:** Government-backed, authoritative valuation data

**What They Offer:**
- ✅ Capital Value (CV) and land value
- ✅ Sales history (price, date)
- ✅ Ownership details (from LINZ)
- ✅ Rental estimate (automated valuation model)
- ✅ Suburb statistics (median prices, days on market)
- ✅ Historical imagery (aerial photos over time)
- ✅ Flood map indicator (basic overlay)
- ✅ Integrated with major real estate portals (Trade Me, Realestate.co.nz)

**What They DON'T Offer:**
- ❌ Zoning or District Plan information
- ❌ Building consent records (even locations)
- ❌ Detailed hazard overlays (liquefaction, erosion, tsunami)
- ❌ Infrastructure connection details
- ❌ Contamination/HAIL register checks
- ❌ Investment cash flow analysis
- ❌ Development potential assessment
- ❌ Any legal protection or liability

**Weaknesses:**
- Surface-level data only (no council GIS integration)
- No actionable recommendations
- Generic reports (not tailored to buyer's situation)
- Rental estimates often inaccurate (AVM limitations)
- No human support or interpretation

**Their Moat:**
- **Government ownership** (perceived authority)
- **Valuation database** (largest in NZ)
- **Portal integrations** (embedded in Trade Me listings)

### Tier 3: Building Inspection Companies

#### 4. House Check NZ, Builder's Crack, etc.
**Product:** Pre-Purchase Building Inspection  
**Price:** $500-$900 NZD (depending on property size)  
**Turnaround:** 2-3 working days

**What They Offer:**
- ✅ Physical inspection of structure, roof, foundations
- ✅ Weathertightness assessment
- ✅ Identification of defects (cracks, rot, leaks)
- ✅ Code compliance observations (visual only)
- ✅ Maintenance recommendations
- ✅ Estimated repair costs
- ✅ On-site testing (moisture meters, thermal imaging)

**What They DON'T Offer:**
- ❌ Title or ownership information
- ❌ Zoning or planning restrictions
- ❌ Hazard overlays (flood, liquefaction, etc.)
- ❌ Sales history or valuation
- ❌ Investment analysis
- ❌ Access to council records
- ❌ Legal protection (unless they offer warranty)

**Weaknesses:**
- Only assesses physical condition (no legal/planning context)
- Inspector quality varies significantly
- Cannot see inside walls (hidden defects possible)
- Expensive as a screening tool (better after offer accepted)
- No forward-looking analysis (development potential, etc.)

**Their Moat:**
- **Physical presence** (can't automate hands-on inspection)
- **Expert judgment** (experienced builders spot issues algorithms miss)
- **Industry accreditation** (NZIBS, BIANZ members)

**Relationship to Us:** **Complementary, not competitive** - customers need BOTH our report (legal/planning) AND their inspection (physical condition)

## What Competitors Offer That We DON'T

### 1. Legal Protection (Council LIM)
**Gap:** We cannot provide legally protected reports under Building Act 2004  
**Impact:** Customers cannot use our report for:
- Final settlement decisions
- Mortgage applications
- Insurance underwriting
- Legal dispute resolution

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Clear disclaimers throughout report
- Position as "screening tool" not "LIM replacement"
- Recommend formal LIM before settlement (we can facilitate ordering)
- Partner with councils to offer bundled discount (our report + LIM = $350 vs $400)

---

### 2. Detailed Building Consent Records (Council LIM)
**Gap:** We only show consent locations, not approval status, CCCs, or plans  
**Impact:** Cannot verify if work was completed legally or to code

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Flag any consent locations as "requires verification"
- Offer to order property file as add-on service ($50-100 markup on council fee)
- Build relationship with council duty planners for expedited requests

---

### 3. Physical Building Inspection (Building Inspectors)
**Gap:** We don't assess structural condition, weathertightness, or defects  
**Impact:** Cannot identify rot, leaks, cracks, or code violations

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Explicitly state this limitation in report
- Partner with building inspectors for referral commissions
- Offer bundled package (our report + their inspection = discount)
- Focus on pre-offer stage (they focus on post-offer)

---

### 4. Mortgage/Credit Data (CoreLogic)
**Gap:** We don't have access to loan indicators, mortgage stress data, or credit-derived insights  
**Impact:** Cannot assess neighborhood financial stress or foreclosure risk

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Not critical for most residential buyers
- CoreLogic's advantage is B2B (banks, insurers), not B2C
- Our investment metrics are sufficient for individual investors

---

### 5. Registered Valuation (QV, Independent Valuers)
**Gap:** We provide estimated market value range, not registered valuation  
**Impact:** Banks won't accept our valuation for mortgage approval

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Clear disclaimer: "Not a registered valuation"
- Provide comparable sales evidence to support estimate
- Partner with registered valuers for referrals when bank valuation needed

---

### 6. Brand Authority & Trust (QV, Councils)
**Gap:** We're unknown startup vs. government SOE or statutory authority  
**Impact:** Harder to convince cautious buyers to trust our data

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Lead with speed and affordability (different value prop)
- Collect testimonials and case studies aggressively
- Partner with trusted real estate agents (their endorsement transfers trust)
- Transparent about data sources (LINZ, council GIS - same as competitors)
- Money-back guarantee if data is materially incorrect
