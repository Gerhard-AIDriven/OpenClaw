💡 Key Insights & Patterns

1. Mutton Products Dominate Volume

The top 5 performers by sales volume are dominated by mutton cuts (ribs, legs, shanks), suggesting strong customer demand for premium mutton products. These products show consistent monthly growth (+0.25 to +0.93 units/month).

2. Margin Expansion Opportunity: Biltong Category

BW Karoo Beef Biltong (#12) stands out with the highest monthly GP improvement (+0.06%/month), translating to +2.99 margin points over the period. This suggests successful pricing power and/or cost management.

3. Sausage & Processed Meat Growth

Bulk boerewors and sausage products show strong quantity growth (+0.82 to +1.67 units/month), indicating growing customer preference for convenience/bulk formats. However, margin expansion is modest (+0.01% to +0.03% monthly).

⚠️ Observation: Most products show quantity growth without proportional margin expansion. This suggests price competition may be constraining gross profit ratio improvements despite higher volume.

4. Products with Mixed Performance

Several products in the 50-month dataset show recent volume increases but stagnant GP ratios. This indicates successful demand generation but potential pricing or cost pressures.
