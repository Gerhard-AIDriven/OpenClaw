# Discovery Questionnaire: SPAR Store AI Readiness
### AI Driven — Confidential
*For use in initial discovery meeting with SPAR store owner*

---

## How to Use This
Don't read these questions off a list — use them as a guide for a natural conversation.
Your goal is to understand their pain, quantify the problem, and build trust.
Listen 70%, talk 30%. Take notes.

---

## Section 1: About the Business

1. How long have you owned this store, and how many staff do you have?
2. What's your approximate number of active SKUs?
3. How many suppliers do you order from regularly?
4. Do you have a dedicated buyer/ordering person, or do you handle that yourself?
5. What other systems do you use alongside IntelliAcc? *(POS, accounting, supplier portals?)*

---

## Section 2: Current Pain Points

6. **On a scale of 1–10, how confident are you in your current ordering process?**
   *(If less than 8 — "What would make it a 10?")*

7. When last did you have a significant stockout on a high-selling item? What happened?

8. Do you have products that sit on the shelf for weeks or months without moving?
   *(Roughly what % of your stock would you say is slow-moving or dead?)*

9. How do you currently decide what to order and how much?
   *(Looking for: gut feel vs system-driven vs supplier rep recommendations)*

10. How much time does your buyer/ordering person spend on placing orders each week?

11. What's your biggest frustration with your current ordering and stock management process?

---

## Section 3: Financial Impact

12. Do you have a sense of how much cash is tied up in stock at any given time?

13. Have you ever had to write off or heavily discount stock because it expired or became unsellable?
    *(Roughly how much per year?)*

14. Have you ever lost a sale because you were out of stock on something a customer wanted?
    *(How often does this happen?)*

15. What would it mean to your cash flow if you could reduce your average stock holding by 15–20%?

---

## Section 4: IntelliAcc & Data

16. How long have you been using IntelliAcc?

17. Which modules do you actively use? *(Stock management, EasyAcc accounting, client retention?)*

18. Do you currently use the reporting features in IntelliAcc?
    *(If yes — which reports? If no — why not?)*

19. Can you export your sales and stock data to Excel or CSV from IntelliAcc?

20. Would you be comfortable sharing 3–6 months of sales and stock data for analysis?
    *(Frame it: "This lets me show you exactly where the opportunities are in your specific store — with your own numbers.")*

---

## Section 5: Appetite for Change

21. Have you tried any technology or tools to improve your ordering process before?
    *(What worked? What didn't?)*

22. How does your team generally respond to new systems or processes?

23. Who else would be involved in a decision like this?
    *(Partner, accountant, store manager?)*

24. If we could show you a clear ROI — for example, freeing up R200k in cash flow or saving 5 hours a week in admin — what would that be worth to you as an investment?

25. What would a successful outcome look like for you in 6 months?

---

## Section 6: Wrap-Up

26. Is there anything about your business I should understand that I haven't asked about?

27. What's your timeline — is this something you'd want to move on quickly, or are you still exploring?

28. What's the best way to stay in touch with you?

---

## After the Meeting — Notes to Capture

- [ ] Their single biggest pain point
- [ ] Estimated financial impact of the problem (stockouts + dead stock + admin time)
- [ ] Decision maker(s) confirmed
- [ ] Data export — are they willing? What format?
- [ ] Timeline and urgency
- [ ] Any red flags or hesitations to address in proposal
- [ ] Agreed next step and date
