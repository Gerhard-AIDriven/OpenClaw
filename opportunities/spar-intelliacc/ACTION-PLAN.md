# Action Plan: SPAR IntelliAcc Prospect Development

## Goal
Convert prospect to paying client — starting with an AI Readiness Assessment,
leading to a full inventory optimisation implementation + monthly retainer.

---

## Stage 1: Research & Preparation
**Timeframe: Before first contact**

- [ ] Find out client's full name and store location
- [ ] Research their specific SPAR store (size, turnover if known, any online presence)
- [ ] Review IntelliAcc EasyAcc module features in detail — know their system better than they do
- [ ] Prepare discovery questionnaire (see DISCOVERY-QUESTIONNAIRE.md)
- [ ] Prepare a 1-page "What AI can do for your SPAR store" overview — simple, visual, no jargon
- [ ] Identify 2–3 relevant case studies (e.g. IntelliAcc Welgedacht SPAR R300k cash flow story)

---

## Stage 2: Initial Contact & Discovery Meeting
**Timeframe: Week 1–2**

- [ ] Make initial contact — warm intro preferred (mutual contact, LinkedIn, or direct)
- [ ] Frame the conversation around their pain, not your solution:
  *"I help SPAR owners use AI to reduce stock holding and prevent stockouts — would you be open to a 30-minute conversation?"*
- [ ] Run discovery meeting using DISCOVERY-QUESTIONNAIRE.md
- [ ] Listen more than you talk — understand their biggest frustration first
- [ ] Request a sample data export (3 months sales + stock data) to prepare a demo
- [ ] Leave behind: 1-page overview + your contact details

---

## Stage 3: Demo & Proposal
**Timeframe: Week 2–3**

- [ ] Analyse their sample data (if received)
- [ ] Build a simple demo using their own numbers — show stockout risks, overstock items, potential cash savings
- [ ] Prepare formal proposal (see PROPOSAL template — to be created)
- [ ] Lead with Phase 1 Assessment as low-risk entry point (R8,000–12,000)
- [ ] Present ROI clearly: what will this cost vs what will it save/generate?
- [ ] Present in person or via video call — not by email

---

## Stage 4: Close & Kick Off
**Timeframe: Week 3–4**

- [ ] Follow up within 48 hours of proposal delivery
- [ ] Handle objections (cost, time, trust, "our system already does this")
- [ ] Sign engagement letter / simple contract
- [ ] Collect deposit (50% upfront recommended)
- [ ] Schedule kickoff meeting
- [ ] Request full data access for Phase 1 Assessment

---

## Stage 5: Deliver Phase 1 — AI Readiness Assessment
**Timeframe: 2 weeks after kickoff**

- [ ] Map their current ordering and stock management process end-to-end
- [ ] Analyse 6–12 months of sales and stock data
- [ ] Identify top 10 slow movers / dead stock items
- [ ] Identify top 10 stockout-risk SKUs
- [ ] Calculate potential cash saving from EOQ optimisation
- [ ] Deliver written report + presentation
- [ ] Propose Phase 2 implementation

---

## Stage 6: Phase 2 — Implementation
**Timeframe: 4–6 weeks**

- [ ] Build forecasting model (demand forecasting per SKU)
- [ ] Set up reorder point alerts
- [ ] Build draft PO automation
- [ ] Train store manager and buyer
- [ ] Handover + documentation

---

## Stage 7: Retainer
**Ongoing from month 2**

- [ ] Monthly check-in and model refinement
- [ ] Expand to more suppliers / SKU categories
- [ ] Add seasonal forecasting
- [ ] Explore next AI opportunity (customer retention, promotions optimisation, etc.)

---

## Key Messages for This Client
- "You already have the data — IntelliAcc captures everything. We just make it work harder for you."
- "This isn't about replacing your team — it's about giving them better information to make faster decisions."
- "Other SPAR owners using smarter inventory management have freed up R300k+ in cash flow."
- "We start small, prove the value, then grow."

---

## Risks & Objections to Prepare For
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "IntelliAcc already does this" | "It captures the data — AI analyses patterns across thousands of SKUs simultaneously and predicts future demand. That's different." |
| "It's too expensive" | "Phase 1 pays for itself if we find R50k in dead stock — and we usually find much more." |
| "I don't have time" | "We do the heavy lifting. You review and approve. One hour a week from you." |
| "I'm not a tech person" | "Neither are most of our clients. We translate the tech into simple recommendations." |
| "What if it doesn't work?" | "Phase 1 is low risk — it's an assessment. If we don't find value, you've lost R10k and gained clarity." |
