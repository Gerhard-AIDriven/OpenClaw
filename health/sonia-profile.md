## Why the Plateau (2 weeks @ 96.2 kg)

**Likely Causes:**
1. **Metabolic adaptation** — body has adjusted to the deficit after 12 kg loss
2. **Tirzepatide efficacy curve** — GLP-1/GIP agonists show diminishing appetite suppression over time; tolerance develops
3. **Insufficient deficit** — current eating patterns may have drifted closer to maintenance (unintentional)
4. **Water retention** — common with medication changes; can mask fat loss for 1–2 weeks
5. **Calorie creep** — portion sizes or snacking may have gradually increased as hunger returns

**Solution:** Reset with structured macro targets + modest activity increase

**Rationale:**

- **High Protein (120–130g):** 
  - Preserves muscle during weight loss (critical at 64yo)
  - Highest satiety per calorie (thermic effect ~25%)
  - GLP-1 agonists already reduce hunger; protein helps with adherence
  - ~1.25–1.35 g/kg body weight (appropriate for deficit + age)

- **Moderate Carbs (145–160g):**
  - Sufficient for activity (6,500 steps) without energy crashes
  - Prioritize **low-GI** sources (oats, brown rice, sweet potato, legumes)
  - Timing: front-load carbs at breakfast/lunch; lighter at dinner
  - Reason: Tirzepatide enhances GLP-1 signaling → improved glucose tolerance

- **Moderate Fat (45–55g):**
  - Still satiating, but controlled to stay within calorie budget
  - Avoid excess (the plateau may have involved high-fat creep)
  - Focus on quality: olive oil, nuts, fatty fish, avocado

## Breaking the Plateau: Action Plan

### Week 1–2: Reset Phase
1. **Track meals precisely** — use MyFitnessPal or Cronometer for 2 weeks minimum
   - Sonia likely doesn't realize current intake (common after initial weight loss)
   - GLP-1 agonists reduce hunger but don't eliminate calorie awareness need

2. **Implement structured meals** — follow the plan above
   - Reduces decision fatigue
   - Makes tracking easier

3. **Protein front-loading** — ensure 40g protein by lunch
   - Stabilizes blood sugar on Tirzepatide
   - Reduces afternoon cravings

4. **Hydration** — 2.5–3L water daily
   - Tirzepatide can cause dehydration (GLP-1 effect on thirst)
   - Water supports GLP-1 efficacy

### Week 3–4: Activity Boost
1. **Increase daily steps to 8,000–10,000**
   - Current 6,500 is good baseline; +1,500 steps ≈ +100 kcal/day
   - Spread across 3–4 short walks (10–15 min each)
   - Easier than formal exercise; Tirzepatide patients often tolerate walking well

2. **Add light resistance (2×/week)**
   - 15–20 min of bodyweight or light dumbbells (3x8–10 reps per exercise)
   - Preserves muscle during weight loss (critical at age 64)
   - Doesn't need to be gym-based; home workouts fine

### Ongoing (Weeks 4+)
1. **Weekly weigh-in** (same day/time, after bathroom)
   - Expect 0.5 kg/week loss if plan is followed
   - Normal water fluctuation: ±1 kg day-to-day (ignore spikes)

2. **Plateau management:**
   - If no loss after 3 weeks → reduce calories to 1,450 kcal (−100 kcal)
   - If loss continues → maintain current targets
   - Reassess every 4 weeks

3. **Tirzepatide optimization:**
   - Current dose (1.5 mg) may be ineffective if tolerance has developed
   - Sonia's doctor can consider:
     - Dosage increase to 2.5 mg (next step in Mounjaro protocol)
     - OR switch to GLP-1 only (semaglutide) if GIP component ineffective
   - **Timeline:** Can discuss with prescriber if no progress by week 4

## Medical Considerations

**Safe for Tirzepatide:**
- Protein intake at 1.25–1.35 g/kg is safe (kidney function normal in most cases)
- Moderate carb intake supports GLP-1 mechanism
- Calorie deficit of 500/day is standard and safe

**Potential Side Effects (Tirzepatide @ 1.5 mg):**
- Nausea (usually subsides by week 4–6)
- Constipation (increase fiber gradually; hydration critical)
- Reduced appetite (feature, not bug—but track macros anyway)
- Dehydration risk (ensure 2.5–3L water/day)

**When to Consult Doctor:**
- Persistent nausea after 4 weeks
- Signs of hypoglycemia (unlikely, but monitor)
- Any medication interactions with other drugs
- Questions about dosage escalation
