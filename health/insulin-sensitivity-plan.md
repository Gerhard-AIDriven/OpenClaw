## Daily Habits That Lower Insulin

### 1. Time-Restricted Eating (TRE) — ADAPTED FOR SHIFTS
- **Target:** 12h+ overnight fast minimum, push to 14h when possible
- **Why:** 14–16h overnight fasts naturally lower insulin and improve sensitivity
- **Reality:** Strict 16h fasts every day not realistic with SPAR shift rotation
- **See:** `health/shift-eating-schedule.md` for full early/late shift eating plans
- **Universal rule:** No eating within 2h of bedtime
- **Black coffee + water allowed during fast**

### 2. Pre-Breakfast Walk (Already Doing ✅)
- 15–20 min before first meal
- Activates GLUT4 transporters → glucose uptake without insulin
- Critical habit — keep doing this religiously

### 3. Post-Meal Walks (Already Doing ✅)
- 10–15 min walk after meals
- Blunts post-meal glucose spike
- Lower spike = less insulin required

### 4. Protein First
- Start meals with protein/fat, save carbs for last
- Improves satiety, lowers post-meal glucose spike

### 5. Reduce Carb Frequency
- Aim for 2 carb-containing meals per day max
- Make snacks protein/fat only (eggs, biltong, cheese, nuts)

## Foods That Improve Insulin Sensitivity

| Category | Best Choices |
|---|---|
| **Proteins** | Eggs, fatty fish (salmon, mackerel), chicken, biltong, Greek yogurt |
| **Fats** | Olive oil, avocado, nuts (almonds, walnuts), olives |
| **Fibre** | Leafy greens, broccoli, cauliflower, berries |
| **Carbs (when needed)** | Sweet potato, lentils, chickpeas, oats, low-GI bread |
| **Spices** | Cinnamon, turmeric, ginger (all improve insulin sensitivity) |
| **Drinks** | Water, black coffee, green tea, apple cider vinegar (1 tbsp pre-meal) |

## Foods to Minimise (Insulin-Spikers)

- ❌ White bread, white rice, pasta
- ❌ Sugary drinks, fruit juice
- ❌ Bananas (high GI when ripe), grapes, mangoes
- ❌ Pastries, biscuits, cake
- ❌ Processed snacks (chips, crackers)
- ⚠️ Limit fruit to berries + 1 lower-GI fruit/day (apple, pear)
