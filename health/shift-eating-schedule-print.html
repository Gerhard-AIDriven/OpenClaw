📋 Universal Rules

✅ DO

  • No eating within 2h of bedtime — non-negotiable
  • Pre-shift walk every day — even 10 min in the dark beats zero
  • Hand snacks at store = protein only (eggs, biltong, cheese, nuts)
  • Pre-prep meals Sunday afternoon (Late Shift weekends)
  • Black coffee, water, herbal tea allowed anytime during fasting
  • Dinner = protein + veg as default. Carbs at breakfast/lunch only.

❌ DON'T

  • Eat carbs after 18:00 (any shift)
  • Grab "just one biscuit" at the store — slippery slope
  • Skip pre-shift walks because of early starts
  • Sacrifice sleep to fit more eating in
  • Snack mindlessly when on shift — hand snacks are planned, not impulse

🎯 Carb Timing Rules

  • Best carb windows: Breakfast + lunch (active hours)
  • Worst carb timing: Evening
  • 2 carb meals per day MAX
  • Total: 150–170g carbs daily

🥡 Store-Friendly Pre-Prepped Foods

Keep these at work for hand snacks:

  • Hard-boiled eggs (2-3 per shift)
  • Greek yogurt cups (Lancewood) + small bag of nuts
  • Biltong / droewors (low-carb, high-protein)
  • Cheese cubes (mature cheddar, feta)
  • Pre-cut veg + hummus
  • Tuna/sardine tins (especially Saturday early-week)
  • Protein shake powder (emergency backup)
  • Apples / pears (low-GI fruit only)
