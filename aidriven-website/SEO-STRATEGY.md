## ✅ On-Page SEO Implemented (index-new.html)

### Meta Tags Added
```html

<meta name="description" content="Fast, affordable property due diligence reports in Napier & Hastings. Get LINZ title data, council rates, liquefaction & flood hazard info in 24-48 hours. From $75 NZD." />
```

**Why these keywords:**
- "Property due diligence" — primary service

- "Liquefaction map Napier" — high-intent local search

```html
<meta name="geo.region" content="NZ-HBK" />
<meta name="geo.placename" content="Napier" />
<meta name="geo.position" content="-39.4902;176.9120" />
<meta name="ICBM" content="-39.4902, 176.9120" />
```

**Why:** Tells Google we serve Hawke's Bay specifically (local SEO boost)

### Structured Data (Schema.org JSON-LD)
Three schema types implemented:

1. **ProfessionalService** — Business info, hours, contact, pricing
2. **Service** — Service type with price range ($75-$200 NZD)
3. **FAQPage** — Common questions (rich snippets in search results)

**Benefits:**
- Rich snippets in Google (stars, prices, FAQs visible in search)
- Better click-through rates
- Voice search optimization (Google Assistant can read this)

### Open Graph + Twitter Cards

## ⚠️ Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Keyword stuffing** — Write naturally, don't force keywords
2. **Ignoring mobile** — 60%+ searches are on phones
3. **Slow page speed** — Users bounce after 3 seconds
4. **No clear CTA** — Every page should guide users to action
5. **Thin content** — Aim for 1500+ words on blog posts
6. **Inconsistent NAP** — Name, Address, Phone must match everywhere
7. **Not tracking** — Install analytics from day one
8. **Expecting overnight results** — SEO takes 3-6 months to gain traction

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Answer "People Also Ask" questions** — Google shows these in search results; write content that answers them directly

2. **Use location modifiers** — "Napier", "Hawke's Bay", "NZ", "New Zealand" throughout content

3. **Update old content** — Refresh blog posts every 6 months with new data

4. **Video content** — YouTube videos rank in Google; create short explainers

5. **Featured snippets** — Structure content to win position zero (bullet lists, tables, direct answers)

6. **Local news jacking** — Comment on local property news (earn backlinks from news sites)

7. **Competitor gap analysis** — See what keywords competitors rank for, target gaps
