## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation

### **Top 5 Risks & Countermeasures:**

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| **Low awareness** | HIGH | HIGH | Content marketing + FB groups + agent partnerships (see plan above) |
| **Trust gap** ("Why trust you over LIM?") | MEDIUM | HIGH | Free samples, testimonials, money-back guarantee, transparent methodology |
| **Price resistance** | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | Emphasize LIM comparison ($49 vs. $400), offer payment plans, bundle discounts |
| **Real estate agent resistance** | LOW | MEDIUM | Position as value-add for their clients, offer commission, free team training |
| **Property Compass competition** | LOW | MEDIUM | Differentiate on local expertise + rates/easements data (they can't match) |

### **Contingency Plans:**

**If Month 1 < 20 reports:**
- Pivot to direct-to-investor sales (skip retail buyers)
- Offer "Founding Member" lifetime discount ($29/month unlimited)
- Intensify real estate partnership outreach (goal: 10 offices)

**If upgrade rate < 15%:**
- Redesign upgrade CTAs (A/B test messaging)
- Add mid-tier product ($65 "Standard Lite")
- Improve Basic Report disclaimer (create more uncertainty → urgency)

**If churn/refunds > 5%:**
- Conduct exit interviews with every refund request
- Add live chat support (pre-purchase questions)
- Improve report clarity (user testing sessions)
