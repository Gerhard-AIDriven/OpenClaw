## 👤 What is a VA?

**VA = Virtual Assistant**

A remote contractor who handles administrative, operational, or specialized tasks for your business. Not an employee (no payroll taxes, no benefits), but works for you part-time on an as-needed basis.

**Think:** Your "right hand" for everything that doesn't require *your* specific expertise.

## 🎓 Training Plan (First 4 Weeks)

### **Week 1: Orientation**
- Day 1: Welcome call (30 min), company vision
- Day 2-3: Read SOPs, watch training videos
- Day 4-5: Shadow you (Zoom screen share)
- Day 6-7: They do tasks while you watch, give feedback

### **Week 2-3: Supervised Execution**
- Handle 50% of emails (you review before sending)
- Post in FB groups (you approve first)
- Daily 15-min check-in call

### **Week 4+: Independence**
- Handle 90% of routine tasks autonomously
- Weekly 30-min sync (review metrics, plan next week)
- You only intervene for exceptions

### **Ongoing Management:**
- **Monday:** Weekly priorities email (you send)
- **Wednesday:** Quick Slack check-in
- **Friday:** Week-in-review report (they send)
- **Monthly:** Performance review + rate adjustment

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Hiring too cheap** ($3-5/hr = unreliable, poor English)  
✅ **Sweet spot:** $10-15/hour for quality Philippines VA

❌ **Not documenting processes** (they can't read your mind)  
✅ **Write SOPs BEFORE hiring** (Loom videos + written steps)

❌ **Micromanaging** (defeats the purpose)  
✅ **Set clear outcomes, let them figure out "how"**

❌ **No backup plan** (VA gets sick/quits)  
✅ **Have secondary VA on standby** (5 hrs/month retainer)

❌ **Underpaying** (good VAs get snapped up)  
✅ **Pay 10-20% above market for your best VA** (retention)
