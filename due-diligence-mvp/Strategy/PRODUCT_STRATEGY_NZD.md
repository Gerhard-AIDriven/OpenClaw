## Executive Summary

AI Driven offers 4 property information products via two channels (WhatsApp + Website), with intelligent routing based on product complexity and customer needs.

**Core Strategic Insight:**
- **LIM Applications:** Loss leader for customer acquisition + market intelligence
- **Due Diligence Reports:** High-margin, defensible products with no direct competition
- **Value Proposition:** We don't just provide data – we provide **clarity, analysis, and recommendations**

## Product Portfolio

### 1. 🏛️ LIM Application + Expert Analysis
**Price:** NZD $179+ (varies by council fee)  
**Positioning:** Loss Leader / Customer Acquisition  
**Delivery:** 5-10 working days  
**Automation:** 30% automated, 70% manual  

**What Customer Gets:**
- LIM application submission to council (we handle paperwork)
- Council LIM report (raw document)
- **10-page Expert Analysis PDF** including:
  - Executive summary (risk rating: Low/Medium/High)
  - Section-by-section translation (council jargon → plain English)
  - Red flag alerts (critical issues, watch items, all-clear)
  - Recommended next steps (builder, lawyer, surveyor referrals)
  - Glossary of technical terms
- 30-minute consultation call included
- Email support for 7 days

**Pricing Model:**
- Our service fee: NZD $69 (fixed)
- Council fee: Actual cost (varies by council, passed through)
- **Total examples:**
  - Napier: $115 council + $69 service = **$184**
  - Hastings: $125 council + $69 service = **$194**
  - Auckland: $270 council + $69 service = **$339**

**Why Customers Choose Us vs. Council Direct:**
| Council Direct | AI Driven LIM+Analysis |
|----------------|------------------------|
| Raw data only | Raw data + **expert interpretation** |
| No guidance | Clear recommendations & next steps |
| Jargon-heavy | Plain English explanations |
| You figure it out alone | 30-min consultation call included |
| ~$115-270 | $184-339 (worth the extra for clarity) |

**Strategic Purpose:**
- ✅ Customer acquisition (low barrier to entry)
- ✅ Market intelligence (we learn council processes, formats, timelines)
- ✅ Trust builder (prove our expertise)
- ✅ Upsell pathway (LIM customers → Standard/Advanced reports)

### 2. 📋 Basic Due Diligence Report
**Price:** NZD $79  
**Positioning:** Impulse Buy / Entry-Level Product  
**Delivery:** Instant (automated)  
**Automation:** 95-100%  

**What Customer Gets:**
- Property identification & legal description
- Land area, zoning, district plan summary
- Ownership history (last 3 sales)
- Capital value & rating valuations (current + historical)
- Basic hazard screening (flood zone Y/N, erosion risk Y/N)
- Rates information (annual rates, arrears status)
- Sales comparison (nearby properties sold)
- 3-5 page automated PDF report

**Data Sources:**
- LINZ (title data, ownership)
- Council rating databases (CV, rates)
- OneStop/QV (sales history)
- NIWA/environmental databases (basic hazards)

**Target Customer:**
- First-home buyers (pre-offer quick check)
- Curious renters/owners (what's my property worth?)
- Investors screening multiple properties
- People who want "just the basics" before committing to full report

**Margin:** 80-90% (cost: ~$5-15 in API fees)

**Value Prop:**
> "Everything you need to know before making an offer – delivered instantly for less than the cost of a builder's coffee run."

## Risk Mitigation

### Risk: Councils Sue or Block Access
- **Mitigation:** Use only publicly available data, official APIs, OIA requests
- **Contingency:** Pivot to user-submitted data + third-party sources

### Risk: Competitors Copy Model
- **Mitigation:** Build brand, customer loyalty, data moat (proprietary analysis frameworks)
- **Contingency:** Rapid innovation, expand product line, lower prices temporarily

### Risk: Regulatory Changes (Privacy Act, Data Use)
- **Mitigation:** Legal review of data sources, privacy policy, terms of service
- **Contingency:** Anonymize data, shift to B2B model (serve real estate agencies)

### Risk: Economic Downturn (Property Market Crashes)
- **Mitigation:** Diversify (rental compliance reports, commercial due diligence)
- **Contingency:** Pivot to distressed asset research, mortgagee sale reports
