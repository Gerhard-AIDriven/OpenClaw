Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Property Due Diligence Reports

Is this a legal Land Information Memorandum (LIM)?

No. This is a Property Due Diligence Report for informational screening purposes only. It is NOT a substitute for a formal LIM issued under the Building Act 2004.

Use this report for: Pre-offer screening, initial due diligence, investment analysis, identifying potential red flags.

Still obtain before settlement: Formal LIM from council, professional building inspection, legal advice, valuation report (if required by lender).

Can I use this report for final settlement?

No. This report does not provide the statutory protections of a formal LIM and should not be relied upon for final settlement decisions.

Think of it as a screening tool – it helps you decide whether to proceed with a property before spending $300-$450 on a full LIM. If the report looks good and you're serious about buying, then order the formal LIM as part of your settlement due diligence.

How accurate is the data?

We source data directly from official databases:

  • LINZ Data Service: Title details, ownership, easements, covenants
  • Council GIS/Rates databases: Zoning, hazards, rates, valuations
  • Ministry for the Environment: HAIL contamination register
  • GNS Science: Natural hazard layers (flood, erosion, liquefaction)

However, we aggregate publicly available data – we don't verify it independently. Always confirm critical information with official sources before making purchase decisions.

What's included in each package?

Basic ($75): Property ID, title details, zoning overview, natural hazards check, infrastructure/services, HAIL screen, building consents summary, rates info. (7 pages)

Standard ($125): Everything in Basic + detailed zoning analysis, development potential assessment, investment metrics, rental yield calculations, cash flow projections, comparable sales analysis. (10-12 pages)

Premium ($200): Everything in Standard + 5-year capital growth forecast, total return projections, risk factor analysis, growth drivers assessment, priority 12-24 hour turnaround. (13-14 pages)

How long does it take to receive the report?

Standard turnaround: 24-48 hours from when we receive your completed property details form.

Premium priority: 12-24 hours (included with Premium package).

Reports are delivered as PDF via email to the address you provide in the order form.

What areas do you cover?

Currently, we focus on Hawke's Bay region (Napier, Hastings, Central Hawke's Bay) as this is where our founder is based and where we have deep local knowledge.

We're actively expanding to other NZ regions. If you're outside Hawke's Bay and interested in our service, contact us and we'll let you know when we launch in your area.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes! We stand behind the quality of our reports. If you're not satisfied with your report for any reason, contact us within 7 days and we'll provide a full refund – no questions asked.

That said, please remember this is a screening tool, not a formal LIM. Make sure you understand the limitations before ordering (see first FAQ above).

Can I get a report on a rural property or lifestyle block?

Yes, but with some limitations. Our automated system works best for residential properties in urban areas with standard titles and council services.

For rural properties, lifestyle blocks, or unusual titles, we can still generate a report, but some data may be incomplete (e.g., wastewater may be septic rather than council-connected, zoning may be more complex). We'll flag any limitations in the report. If the property is highly unusual, we may recommend a formal LIM instead.
